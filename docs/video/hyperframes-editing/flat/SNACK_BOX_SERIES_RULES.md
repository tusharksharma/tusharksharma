# Split Plate Snack Box Series Rules

## Positioning

- Build higher-protein snack boxes with practical, better-for-you ingredients and no compromise on taste.
- Default every Snack Box batch to 4 boxes: 1 box per serving, covering 2 children for 2 days.
- Position each box for post-lunch or after-school snacking. Weekend use and adult use may be mentioned when relevant.
- Keep portions age-appropriate and state that families can adjust portions for their children.
- Present macros per finished box. Treat nutrition as an estimate based on the specific labels and portions used.
- Count every included component, including dips and sauces, in the per-box macros.

## Video

- Use the title format `Snack Box Episode N: [Name]`.
- Open on moving finished-box action, a crunch, a lift, or another immediate payoff. Never open on a static plate or title card.
- Keep the portrait source fully framed at 1080x1920. Do not crop or artificially zoom.
- Show each ingredient entering the box and give ingredient beats enough time to read naturally.
- Put the ingredient name and portion on screen while that ingredient is shown.
- Use transparent white or yellow text with a black outline in the upper-middle safe area. Do not use solid orange text boxes.
- Show calories and protein per box near the opening and/or finished-box payoff.
- Preserve original food and packing sounds beneath narration. Do not add music; music is added in the posting app.
- Apply restrained food color correction: approximately +5% contrast and +5-10% saturation when the footage benefits from it.

## Marcus Narration

- Use `Marcus - Bright, Upbeat and Clear` with ElevenLabs `eleven_v3`.
- Draft the narration and frame plan for approval before generating or mixing Marcus.
- Align every spoken ingredient and claim with the matching visual. Do not allow narration to finish far ahead of the video.
- Keep the delivery conversational, emotionally varied, and intelligible. Never accelerate isolated phrases enough to sound rushed.
- Mention that exact quantities are in the description and on `thesplitplate.com` when useful.
- End with a varied, specific question that encourages comments. Favor comments and shares over a generic request for likes.

## Website Handoff

- Default deliverables are the TikTok video, Marcus audio, timed script, recipe handoff, structured recipe JSON, social copy, source/still manifest, and a zip package.
- Include yield, per-box portions, assembly instructions, storage guidance, substitutions, macro assumptions, allergens, and diet tags.
- Independently audit allergens for every Snack Box from the complete ingredient stack and current packaged-food labels; ask Tushar only when an unidentified product could materially change the result.
- Verify packaged-food nutrition and allergens from current labels or manufacturer sources. Tell readers to recheck their own packaging because formulations can change.
- When a filmed portion is later corrected, use the corrected portion for the recipe and macros. Mention the filmed variation only when it adds useful, honest context.
- Use generic ingredient names in the main recipe. Mention brands only when they materially affect macros, flavor, or the footage context.
