## Standard Base

- 1 cup Fairlife 2% ultra-filtered milk
- 2 tbsp monk fruit/erythritol sweetener
- 1/2 serving Opportuniteas unflavored whey isolate (60 calories, 14g protein; user-confirmed label)
- 1/8 tsp guar gum, leveled
- 1 pinch salt

### Base change approved October 8, 2026

- Tushar is already hitting his protein goal easily, so half a serving of whey is the usual base going forward. Keep the other base ingredients unchanged unless he specifies another change.
- Recalculate each complete pint from this base; never carry forward the previous full-serving whey macros.
- Do not claim reduced whey causes improved texture. Tushar liked the cinnamon-roll build but did not establish what caused that result.
- Do not announce week numbers in new videos. Use “this week” when relevant and name the actual cookie flavor.

## Standard Processing

1. Blend the standard base until smooth.
2. Add flavor-specific ingredients at the stage confirmed for that recipe.
3. Freeze upright and level for 24 hours.
4. Rest the frozen pint on the counter for 5 minutes. The default method does not use a hot-water bath.
5. Run Regular Ice Cream, not Lite Ice Cream.
6. Run Respin without adding liquid after the first Regular Ice Cream cycle. This is the saved default and should not be reconfirmed for every flavor.
7. When a flavor has a machine-processed mix-in, add it after the first spin and use Respin under the no-water method unless the user gives a flavor-specific correction.
8. Add post-scoop garnishes after all machine processing.
9. Flavor emulsions default to `1/4 tsp` unless Tushar explicitly specifies a different amount.
