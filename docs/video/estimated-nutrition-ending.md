# Estimated nutrition ending — confirmed October 23, 2026

For future long-form recipe films, show a readable nutrition summary after the cooking/story payoff and before the final watch-next card. Include estimated calories, macronutrients and supported micronutrients. Show one rounded point estimate for each nutrient, never a range. Label the card **Estimated nutrition** and state the serving basis clearly, including whether bread, rice, sauces and sides are included.

Calculate from the actual ingredient quantities, relevant package labels and reliable nutrient composition data. Record sources, yield, portion calculation and assumptions in the private episode record. Use appropriate units and rounding; a single estimate is not a laboratory measurement. Do not invent missing micronutrient values or turn missing data into zero. Resolve material ingredient/portion uncertainty before final approval. Do not silently borrow numbers for a different serving or version.

Make the card legible at phone size, splitting the information across concise cards if necessary. The nutrition summary is separate from the appended native watch-next outro, which remains five seconds maximum. Avoid extending a static outro to accommodate the numbers; keep the film's closing deliberate and brief. This rule applies going forward and does not regenerate approved films or authorize publication.

## Moving picture and music — confirmed October 26, 2026

Keep the estimated-nutrition overlay on screen for about **7–8 seconds**, with relevant real cooking/finished-food video continuing behind it and saved channel music playing underneath. Use a compact readable overlay; do not freeze the background or replace it with an opaque full-screen page. Choose useful food action, preserve its natural speed and avoid looping or unnecessarily replaying the main film. Keep numbers/serving basis legible during that short hold and let the music continue smoothly into the separate watch-next ending, which is still five seconds maximum. Use existing authorized music; this rule does not authorize new paid music or narration. Apply to the current meatball revision and future recipe films; preserve already approved earlier releases.
