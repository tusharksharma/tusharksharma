# The Split Plate — standing food principles

These principles are explicit direction from Tushar. Use them when writing recipes, scripts, stories and brand copy. Preserve their practical meaning; they do not authorize unsupported nutrition, timing, taste or family claims.

## Home-cooked includes convenience

Added October 11, 2026:

> Anything cooked at home is a home-cooked meal, even frozen.
> Work hard on the star.

A meal prepared at home can include frozen, packaged and ready-to-heat ingredients. Give those shortcuts credit as part of getting dinner made. Put effort where it matters most for that meal—the star of the plate—and let straightforward sides be straightforward. Do not apologize for convenience or imply that every component must be made from scratch.

This adds to the shared-cook philosophy: adapt a meal to the people eating it without making different preferences a moral judgment. Describe the actual choices in the household; do not turn one evening into a universal parenting recommendation.
