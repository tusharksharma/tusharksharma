## Short introduction

I'm Tushar, the person behind The Split Plate. I build practical ways to make high-protein eating work alongside family dinners. One shared cook can become different plates, portions or finishes without making everyone's preferences a problem. Convenience ingredients belong in that system, and taste matters enough that I'll try small changes—like warming a protein brownie—to make something more enjoyable.

Editorial wording based on the confirmed brand principles and experiences below. It does not establish an origin date, qualification, transformation story or professional nutrition credential.

## Longer introduction for future scripts

I'm Tushar. The Split Plate is where I share the meals, shortcuts and food experiments that fit my family life and my own protein goals. I care about a system I can repeat: one shared cooking process, flexible plates and effort spent where it makes the biggest difference.

I count convenience ingredients as part of home cooking. Frozen ingredients, prepared components and simple sides can all earn their place. Adult protein goals don't become rules for the children's dinner, and eating together doesn't require identical plates.

I also like testing whether a small change actually makes food better. Warming a Prime Bites brownie is a big improvement for me; warming Barebells or Quest hasn't made much difference in my experience. Those specific tradeoffs are the kind of thing I want to share, rather than treating every protein product or kitchen trick as a winner.
