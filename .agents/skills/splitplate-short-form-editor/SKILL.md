## Choose the task boundary

- Rules or agent maintenance: update instructions only. No footage processing, voice generation, renders or publishing.
- Review: inspect the specified version and report timestamped findings; do not silently recut an approved export.
- Edit or recut: preserve originals and resume existing project state. A specific edit does not restart a full creative interview.
- New recipe short: record recipe source, exact build/serving, requested cuts, platform, footage and approved narrative. Use the house format; do not impose an adult/kid split on recipes without one.

Check actual footage, installed renderer/version, ffmpeg/ffprobe, fonts, source color, provider access and costs before promising execution. Treat Marcus `eleven_v4` as the supplied preference: verify availability at use time, report unavailable configuration and do not silently substitute a voice/model. ## Editing sequence

1. **Allocate evidence.** Before assigning raw ranges, read the canonical UNUSED_CLIP_INVENTORY linked in WORKSPACE_AGENTS. Save a source-file/in/out map spanning all required companion cuts and reserves. Apply dinner-package and six-or-more-ingredient comment-cut rules where relevant. Missing ledger access can leave independent planning possible, but cannot justify claiming verified allocation or a complete package.
2. **Audition the opening.** Record at least two available raw events and inspect motion at phone size with and without audio. Open on genuine moving food proof within the first second. Keep recipes, brands, family statements and calorie/protein estimates grounded in the actual build and serving basis.
3. **Prepare approval-ready narrative.** Present hook, arc, exact Marcus script, tone and one closing question before voice generation or final rendering. Honor explicit prior approval of this exact narrative. A recipe, outline or approval of a different package does not substitute. Compact Creami/comparable recipe-review cuts target about 20 seconds maximum including the cover; this is not a universal duration. Keep concrete Base/Mix-in/Topping stages readable when used; remove public week/episode numbering.
4. **Build picture and text.** Preserve original food/skin color and clarity. Avoid persistent full-frame scrims, routine grades or saturation boosts. Make a deliberate HDR/SDR choice; technical tone mapping must be verified. Use local text backing and safe placement. Sync captions to the final paced narration. Keep exact quantities and the full method in the posting recipe. Follow core timing contracts and animate inner wrappers, not framework-owned clip visibility.
5. **Mix and cover.** Make a per-cut music decision using the user's actual tracks, preserving genuine sensory sound at its action time. Judge decoded sound, not fader values. Do not fabricate texture/reaction evidence with sound effects. Produce the required cover options with truthful ImageGen-polished photography; select bold by default, preserve a standalone image and append approximately 0.3 seconds (nine frames at 30fps). Reuse approved assets when appropriate; expression changes require the specified likeness review.
6. **Prove the export path.** Follow the house fast-path guidance before expensive full renders. Inspect a short encoded proof through the same intended pipeline, especially native HDR and nested text. Check composition timelines, timed inner panels, graphics rows and source keyframe spacing when relevant. Do not dismiss preview/export differences as harmless warnings. Preserve project-specific fixes and approved originals.
7. **Review and deliver.** Copy the complete source QA checklist into the project as QA.md for every publishable cut; record evidence and failures. Obtain the required finished-preview approval before final rendering. Handle Spanish language QA separately from Tushar's visual/pacing review. Inspect the actual encoded file: motion, caption overlap/timing, opening and CTA, food color against raw/prepared frames, audio audibility/headroom, full decode, freezes, dimensions and exact cover tail. Technical validation alone is insufficient.

Before calling a package Ready, satisfy all applicable cover, caption, manifest, inventory, posting-ledger and reserve-clip gates with readbacks. Export real reserves and record verified paths; never fabricate rows to close a gate. If a gate cannot be met, preserve completed work and report the specific missing evidence or requested exception. Creating an edit does not authorize social publishing or scheduling.
