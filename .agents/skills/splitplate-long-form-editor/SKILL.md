## Picture first, narration against it

1. Inspect original source metadata, orientation, recorded speech and cooking coverage. Save a source manifest and editable source-file/in/out timeline outside the public repo. Keep camera originals recoverable.
2. Establish the film's actual subject, audience promise, requested duration and one story arc. Ask for the personal details that change the narration; continue independent picture work while answers are pending. Do not invent memories, reasons, verdicts, child reactions, quantities or cooking temperatures.
3. Build an intentional picture assembly. Remove camera setup, unnecessary phone checking, aimless movement and repeated waiting. Preserve meaningful process and genuine sensory moments. The requested runtime is a target to satisfy through useful material, not looping or filler.
4. Frame around the food and active hands to reduce clutter. Confirmed October 22, 2026: for future wide-angle XTRA Atto POV footage, start with a gentle total 1.10x-1.15x digital crop from the original camera file, adjusted per shot. Include any existing crop/stabilization in that total; do not stack another zoom onto an already cropped proxy. Preserve the pan, ingredients, plates and hand movement; widen or reduce the crop when needed. Keep the requested 4K landscape and measured 59.94/60 fps delivery, but do not call digital zoom lossless: a 4K crop resampled to 4K gives up some native detail. Inspect representative moving encoded shots at full size against the originals for softness, noise and clipped action; prefer a wider frame when the crop hurts clarity. This is a future default, not a retrofit of approved exports. Native landscape footage can fill the frame without portrait side panels.
5. Write narration against source-backed actions and chapter timings. Explain what is happening, why consequential steps matter when the reason is known, and how the confirmed household story fits. Let real sound breathe. Do not read the same ingredient list repeatedly or describe an action at the wrong point in the film.
6. Present the exact script, emotional arc and closing action before paid voice generation. Carry forward explicit approval for unchanged text. Keep unconfirmed claims out of the approved read; do not substitute a fictional story merely to fill the voice track.

## Color and export

Preserve natural food and skin color. Inspect source transfer/primaries/bit depth before deciding output. Never turn SDR into HDR by retagging or claim that conversion restores uncaptured highlights. When the user requests HDR-compatible delivery from SDR, apply a deliberate technical transform and label provenance as SDR-derived. When native HDR exists, preserve it through a verified pipeline.

Rebuild the final master from original media or verified full-quality intermediates; do not upscale the review proxy as the master. Honor rotation once. Test a short representative export before an expensive full render. Record resolution, frame rate, crop and color decisions in the project, with the user's requested settings distinguished from the source characteristics.

Confirmed October 12, 2026: for Tushar's Atto footage recorded at 4K, 16:9 and 60 fps, preserve 3840×2160, landscape 16:9 and the original approximately 59.94/60 fps in future long-form masters unless he requests a different delivery. Do not silently reduce it to 30 fps. Match the actual measured source rate; slow-motion treatment is a separate editing choice. Resolution and frame-rate preservation do not imply HDR recording or brighter grading.

Inspect moving encoded picture and sound at the opening, every important action, chapter transitions, payoff and end. Verify full decode, dimensions, duration, pixel format, color tags, caption timing, speech intelligibility and headroom. Check crops through each selected range. A technical pass or contact sheet alone does not establish audiovisual quality. HDR screenshots and ordinary SDR playback do not prove actual HDR display behavior; review an intentional SDR companion and HDR playback where available.

For SDR-derived HDR brightness checks and a complete YouTube upload package, read [Export and publishing lessons](references/export-and-publishing.md). Compare the platform's actual playback with the source; metadata and 4K availability alone do not prove brightness or HDR-display quality.
