# Automatic long-form promotion

Confirmed by Tushar on October 12, 2026. After each authorized long-form YouTube publication, automatically continue into this promotion workflow as part of the same job. Do not wait for a separate request to prepare and distribute the package. This is an event-driven production rule, not a timer or proof of unattended account access. Rules-only work and unpublished edits do not trigger posting.

## Destinations

- YouTube: publish or schedule genuine Shorts with the matching full film attached as Related video. Shorts are welcome on the channel.
- Facebook: publish a link post with the full YouTube URL, the film's thumbnail/link preview and a short personal caption. Do not substitute a native Reel or video post.
- Instagram: Stories only, with a native clickable link sticker to the full YouTube video. No promotional feed posts or Reels for this workflow. Preserve the profile grid.
- TikTok: Stories only. Do not upload a normal feed video as a workaround. Mention a profile link only when its actual destination has been verified; do not imply the Story contains a clickable link when it does not.

These constraints apply to promotion of long-form films, not to independently requested short-form recipe videos. Do not silently expand into other networks, direct messages, paid boosts or profile/account changes.

## Production and execution

Choose distinct, footage-backed moments and a single invitation to the full film. Reuse approved narration when it fits; obtain exact-script approval and a stage-specific credit cap for any new paid generation. Reuse an approved thumbnail or make format-appropriate covers through the short-form workflow. Preserve source color and measured frame rate, uncluttered crops, readable captions and genuine action sound. Keep newly invented personal claims out.

Verify the current film's camera style before using POV in graphics, captions, descriptions or hashtags. Owning or previously using an Atto does not make every film POV. Tushar confirmed the golden garlic rice and steak film was not POV; its promotion must omit that label.

Honor the short-form finished-preview gate for newly edited clips. An unchanged, already approved asset does not need another approval loop merely because it is shared to another authorized destination. Standing distribution authorization applies after the actual film's publication is authorized; it does not authorize publishing the long film itself.

Use an approved rollout or the current content calendar; avoid posting all angles at once. October 15 and 18 at 5 p.m. Pacific were approved for the remaining two steak-night promos only, not a permanent schedule. For future films, set film-relative dates against the actual calendar and explain the selected rollout. Honor current user changes.

Verify the intended Split Plate account, upload type, visibility, destination link and platform checks. Use supported connectors or the actual browser/app composer. If Stories, link stickers or scheduling are unavailable through current tools, preserve the ready asset and provide a precise handoff; never post to the feed as a substitute. Login/verification is a user handoff when necessary.
