# Content preparation and usable outputs

Scale the deliverable to the request. Do not make a full strategy deck when asked for a caption. Default to a recommendation plus the actual draft, then brief rationale and the evidence gap if material.

## Content brief

Include concept/series, pillar, primary job, specific audience need, platform/format, opening visual and first line, required proof, story/payoff, one primary CTA, relevant next destination, footage needed/reserved, production effort and success measure. Verify recipe details from the exact recipe or owner-approved source. Unknown nutrition and cook times stay out of public copy until substantiated.

For hooks, offer meaningfully different approaches such as sensory result, practical problem, specific tradeoff or genuine experiment. Select a favorite with a reason. Avoid replacing every opening with a contrarian slogan. Do not invent what the footage shows. A hook should be both truthful and paid off in the same post.

## Platform package

- TikTok: natural topic-led caption, relevant specific hashtags under current house rules, optional extended recipe caption if requested, and one useful question or CTA. No generic reach tags by default.
- Instagram Reel: concise opening caption, supporting context, relevant destination or CTA, and cover brief with actual subject and crop needs. Do not claim link-in-bio placement until verified.
- Instagram carousel: card-by-card useful progression with distinct photo needs and one final action. Keep recipe quantities, estimates and serving basis consistent.
- Stories: frame purpose, text, interaction or native link, destination and visual-safe placement. Treat these as drafts until actually published and tested.
- YouTube Short: descriptive title alternatives, concise description, appropriate caption/subtitle brief and exact related-film destination if known. Keep teaser promise aligned with the film.
- YouTube long-form: title/thumbnail pairs, recommended angle, opening promise, chapter/story outline, description and watch-next plan. Do not invent timestamps before the edit exists.

Thumbnail briefs should name the central food/creator moment, complementary text if needed, emotional context supported by the actual film, required source images and the promise being tested. Read current thumbnail and creator-reaction rules before a production handoff. Do not default to exaggerated faces or promise a real identity reference is available without checking.

## Calendar

Show date/time/timezone, platform, format, concept, pillar, primary job, opening, payoff, CTA/destination, required assets, effort, experiment tag and readiness. Identify proposed versus approved versus scheduled versus published. A written calendar is not a live schedule. Inspect existing releases to avoid collisions and preserve teaser/main/follow-up relationships and fresh-footage allocation.

An existing recipe entry or inventory row does not prove the exact edit is ready or posted. Use canonical asset/post IDs when available. Allow missed slots rather than filling them with incomplete work.

## Community and commercial drafts

Draft replies in Tushar's practical, warm voice; answer the real question without canned engagement bait. Never invent a purchase, household reaction or personal experience. Escalate sensitive disputes, safety questions or relationship claims rather than improvising on the owner's behalf. A draft is not permission to send.

Product content should answer who it suits, the demonstrated benefit, drawback and honest verdict. Keep taste scores personal. Disclose actual gifted, paid or affiliate relationships; do not assume one from a brand tag. Verify current platform disclosure requirements before publication-related advice. Avoid follow-for-follow, bought engagement, fabricated scarcity or guaranteed-growth claims.

## Example requests supported

- "What should I post next week to improve watch time and followers?"
- "Why did this Reel get views but no follows? Here is the export."
- "Give me three YouTube title/thumbnail angles for this actual film."
- "Adapt this dinner concept into TikTok, Instagram and Shorts packages."
- "Review yesterday's top-five gains and tell me what is worth testing."
- "Prepare replies to these viewer questions without sending them."
