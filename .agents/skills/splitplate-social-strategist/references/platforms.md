# Platform playbooks

These are editorial decision guides, not fixed ranking recipes. Verify current limits, feature access and monetization rules before advising on them. See research.md for primary sources.

## YouTube long-form

Start with a clear viewing promise and an actual story that earns the requested length. Develop title and thumbnail concepts together: title names the question or benefit, thumbnail supplies complementary visual proof. Avoid recipe numbers, dates or generic curiosity that hide the subject. Give several materially different packaging angles and select one with a reason.

The opening should fulfill the click promise quickly, show what is at stake and make the intended payoff clear. Match narration to visible actions. Remove repeated mechanics while preserving cooking comprehension, personality and sensory moments. Do not recommend lengthening a film merely to inflate watch time or reach an assumed monetization threshold.

Diagnose with impressions and CTR by traffic source, opening retention, average view duration, average percentage viewed, total watch time, subscriber gains and returning viewers where available. Low impressions alone do not establish a thumbnail problem. High CTR with early abandonment suggests a promise/story mismatch; inspect the actual opening. Compare similar lengths and viewer sources. A retention dip may be a completed answer or a confusing section; watch it before prescribing cuts.

Plan a relevant watch-next destination and native end screen. Follow the approved long-form intro/CTA treatment even when a generic strategy guide would suggest another approach. Use native title/thumbnail experiments where available; avoid claiming a sequential before/after change proves causality.

## YouTube Shorts

Give a Short a complete useful payoff. Use a clear subject and immediate visual action, understandable sound-off text where useful, and a reason to explore the channel. A promo should give value while inviting the exact corresponding long film, rather than withholding its only payoff.

Assess engaged views, stayed-to-watch versus swiped-away, retention, average duration/percentage and subscriber attribution where available. Public view starts/replays do not equal engaged views or watch time. Keep Shorts and long-form baselines separate. Loops can raise percentage viewed above 100%; this alone does not establish satisfaction.

Use the native Related video destination when appropriate and available. Verify its actual target after publication in a publishing task. Do not claim a caption URL is equivalent to a native clickable control, or promise that Shorts viewers will convert to long-form viewers. Measure that path separately.

## TikTok

Use practical demonstrations, specific opinions, honest experiments and product-use verdicts as distinct reasons to watch. Open with the result, real problem or a supported disagreement; show the proof and finish the thought. Keep the creator's voice natural. Do not manufacture family conflict, outrage or unsupported health comparisons.

Research specific viewer questions using native search suggestions and Creator Search Insights where available. Place the actual topic naturally in speech, on-screen text and the caption when useful. Do not stuff keywords or treat broad hashtags as a distribution guarantee. Apply current Split Plate caption conventions; their hashtag count is a house preference, not an algorithm fact.

Evaluate watch time, completion relative to runtime, retention, shares, profile visits and post-attributed follows when available. The transfer's view counters cannot supply all these fields. A popular old tip gaining views is a topic lead, not proof the next upload should be identical.

For unusually low reach, inspect processing, visibility, recommendation eligibility, policy notices, rights and novelty before assigning a cause. Read the existing Reelrise learning model for the active repeated-footage hypothesis; keep it labeled a hypothesis. Avoid immediate duplicate reuploads. Trends must fit the actual food, audience and rights available to the account.

## Instagram

Use Reels for clear demonstrations and personality, carousels for useful reference instructions or comparisons, and Stories for context and conversation around real activity. Adapt the packaging to the format rather than posting every asset everywhere. A thumbnail must remain legible in relevant crops and match the actual content.

Reels should deliver one understandable promise with visible evidence. Evaluate watch time and retention where available, non-follower reach, shares/saves per reach, profile visits and attributed follows. Saved recipes and shared opinions can serve different goals; avoid optimizing every post for saves. Compare carousels separately from video.

Make carousels usable: specific first-card promise, concise progressive steps, distinct relevant imagery, complete payoff and one CTA. Follow current cover/carousel image rules before execution. Use Stories for a real question, a short explanation or a relevant native link. A burned-in label is not a clickable sticker. During publishing, test the visible sticker center and exact destination in the actual viewer, per the promotion playbook.

Trial Reels can help explore an idea with non-followers when available on the account. Check current account eligibility and feature behavior. Treat results as a useful directional test, not a randomized experiment or guaranteed reach. Do not silently enable automatic sharing.

## Cross-platform preparation

Begin from a clean owned-footage master, check audio rights separately for every platform and distinguish creator/business account restrictions. Use captions and titles suited to the viewer context. One licensed in-app sound does not establish permission for all exports or commercial uses. Check current disclosure requirements for sponsorship and synthetic content when relevant. Do not classify all AI-assisted editing as the same disclosure case.

Preserve genuinely new footage for separate teasers, mains and follow-ups where the brand rules require it. Do not propose reposting another creator's work as a shortcut; attribution does not itself grant usage rights.
