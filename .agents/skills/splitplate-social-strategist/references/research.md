## Maintenance review — November 1, 2026

These are newly verified additions to this skill's October 22 research record, not a claim that the platforms announced new policies this month.

### YouTube counter definitions across formats

YouTube's August 19, 2026 announcement says that from August 24, first-frame starts count as views across formats, including long-form; the prior measure becomes engaged views. Most analytics, including CTR, average view duration and retention, remain anchored on engaged views, and the announcement says recommendation behavior is unchanged. Before comparing periods or reporting follower conversion, record the exact exported metric and its definition. Do not assume the API or a third-party tracker has adopted a particular counter solely from a public-view label. Apply this distinction to long-form as well as Shorts; a rise in starts does not itself demonstrate more meaningful viewing.
Source, fetched November 1: https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/

### Longer Shorts and claimed audio

YouTube's current help page allows Shorts up to three minutes, but says a Short longer than one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked worldwide. For a longer excerpt, confirm the actual audio rights and claim status before recommending it as a public asset. A long-form film being playable does not establish that an excerpt with the same claimed music is playable as a Short. Check each track's permitted length rather than assuming three minutes of music.
Source, fetched November 1: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877

### AI disclosure is about the actual alteration

YouTube distinguishes realistic, meaningful AI alterations from minor aesthetic edits. Its examples requiring disclosure include making a real person appear to say something they did not say, a realistic invented scene, and generated music that is the video's main focus. Assess the particular narration, image or music treatment and current upload controls; do not infer a blanket exemption from real cooking footage or label every minor polish operation identically.
Source, fetched November 1: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491

### TikTok disclosure and publishing-route scope

The fetched Direct Post API guidelines distinguish promotion of one's own business from third-party branded content, with different disclosure labels and music-confirmation handling. Assess the actual relationship and content purpose, including own-business promotion; a brand mention alone does not establish sponsorship. These are integration requirements, not proof that the native app uses identical controls. Do not translate API-specific watermark rules into a blanket ban on a creator's own branding. A selected native sound does not establish cross-platform rights.
Source, fetched November 1: https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-sharing-guidelines
Access limitation: TikTok commercial-music and promotion Help Center pages redirected to inaccessible FAQ endpoints. Search summaries were not accepted as a fresh full-policy verification. Recheck current in-app/official music terms for a particular commercial post.
