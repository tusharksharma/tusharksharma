# Measurement and experimentation

## Metric contract

Use platform, stable post ID, format, publication time, capture time, elapsed age, primary job and source. Record duration, views and their definition, reach, watch time and units, average view duration, retention, shares, saves, substantive comments, profile visits, follows/subscribers and production minutes only where present. Missing stays missing; zero means a measured zero.

Confirm units from the collector or provider definition. In the reviewed Instagram collector, average watch time is milliseconds converted to seconds by dividing by 1000. Do not divide again if an export is already in seconds. Do not estimate total watch time as views times average duration unless the populations and metric definitions match, and label any valid derivation.

Compare within platform, format, purpose, length band and age window. Use seven-day results when available, supplemented by early observations clearly labeled provisional. An archive of daily cumulative snapshots does not necessarily contain an exact seven-day observation. Record nearest capture age and avoid implying precision. Use medians and distributions; show the outlier separately. Separate organic and paid results.

Follower conversion can mean attributed follows / reached accounts, or attributed follows / views when reach is unavailable. Name the denominator and optionally express per 1,000. YouTube subscriber gains per 1,000 engaged Shorts views is another metric with a different denominator. Do not compare these as if identical. Net account growth includes unfollows; it is not the sum of post-attributed follows. Never invent attribution from account-level changes.

Watch-time efficiency can compare measured watch minutes or attributed followers per production hour, provided the observation window and source are stated. For long-form, track total watch hours alongside AVD and audience retention. Avoid combining platform watch time into one number unless definitions and scope are compatible.

## View-gain history

The supplied transfer ranks observed counter increase between successive successful complete captures, regardless of when a post was published. It is a momentum report, not a same-age content experiment or a watch-time/follower report. Show actual interval start/end and hours. Unequal intervals should not be called equal daily comparisons; an hourly normalization can aid context but does not remove time-of-day or audience confounding.

Use zero baseline only for a genuinely new post published within the interval. Exclude or flag missing baselines, negative corrections, absent current posts, incomplete/filtered/limited pulls and invalidated snapshots. Preserve source history. Unknown YouTube Shorts classifications remain excluded. A missing cell in the top-five matrix means not in that run's top five, not zero growth.

## Diagnosing results

Inspect metric availability before declaring a cause. Distinguish weak initial distribution, weak opening, unfulfilled promise, insufficient payoff and weak follow proposition. Provide two plausible explanations when evidence cannot discriminate. Use the actual post/video or retention curve when prescribing an edit; a caption alone does not establish what the opening showed.

Check whether existing experiments are active in `docs/video/REELRISE_LEARNING_MODEL.md` before starting another. Test one major variable at a time. A useful experiment record contains hypothesis, audience/format, control or baseline, changed variable, repeated examples, reasonably stable factors, observation ages, primary metric, guardrail and review rule. Set a practical review point based on posting capacity, not a universal minimum sample size.

Example: test result-first versus problem-first openings across comparable kitchen tips. Primary metric: retention at the earliest available comparable point; guardrail: follower conversion and usefulness. If retention is unavailable, use a stated proxy and do not call it a retention test. Publish variants with distinct footage/story where novelty rules require it. Do not upload visually equivalent versions solely to manufacture an A/B test.

Conclusions can be repeat, revise, stop or insufficient evidence. Promote a lesson only after repeated comparable support and no conflicting owner instruction. Record what would change the conclusion.
